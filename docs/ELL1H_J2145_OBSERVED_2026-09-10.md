## Meaning and limitations

No additional circular signal crossed the frozen threshold in the eligible
period range. This is not evidence that J2145 has no planets outside the
searched periods, masked cells or demonstrated sensitivity.

The [calibration](ELL1H_J2145_CALIBRATION_2026-09-10.md) used 4,096 nulls and a
conditional 1% allowance for one grid. Its 90/96 diagnostic injection recoveries
and median worst-phase 95% amplitude of 0.685459 microseconds remain preserved.
They were not rerun for this launch. Sensitivity is period-dependent, on-grid
and conditional on fixed covariance and local linear timing projection. The
absence of a coarse noise flag does not validate all possible correlated or
chromatic noise models. No new companion is confirmed.
