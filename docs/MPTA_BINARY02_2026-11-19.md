## The 21 holds

Held means not searched; it is not a non-detection.

| Cause | Targets | What would clear it |

| T2 orbit model not supported by the timing library | J0101−6422, J2229+2643 | Translating T2 to a supported model |

J1125−5825, J1751−2857, J1455−3330 and J2229+2643 have inner periods of
76–111 days; with the ten-inner-period rule their search range would start
above 400 days, so they could not be searched in this range even if cleared.
The exact failing parameter and numbers for each target are in
`closeout.json`.

## The approved list as a whole

Thirty-two targets: **7 searched, all `NO_TRIGGER`; 25 held.** The seven add
millisecond pulsars sensitive to roughly 0.0005–0.015 M⊕ at 30–400 days. The
linear treatment of the inner orbit clears only simple ELL1/ELL1H models;
most of the list needs one of the three pieces of new support in the table
above. None of that support was built here.
