# UTMOST-NS timing-product request — unsent draft

Prepared 9 September 2026. No message has been sent and no project membership
request has been submitted. Sending requires the owner's instruction.

**To:** Corresponding author of Dunn et al. (2025)

**Subject:** Combined EW+NS timing products for an independent periodicity study

Dear Dr Dunn,

I am conducting a private study of periodic signals in isolated-pulsar timing
data and would like to use the observations underlying *First results from the
UTMOST-NS pulsar timing programme* (MNRAS 541, 1792; arXiv:2506.22697).

I can retrieve the single-channel MONSPSR_TIMING arrival times through an
authenticated Pulsar Portal account and have obtained the published NS clock
file. The linked ephemeris records inspected so far do not supply a complete
combined timing solution for our eligible isolated targets. The portal file
index also reports that my account lacks permission.

Could you point me to, or provide, the analysis-ready products underlying the
paper? A representative pair, J0134−2937 and J1327−6222, would be enough to check
our ingestion before requesting the remaining usable isolated-pulsar sample.
The useful items would be:

- Original full-precision combined EW/NS PAR and TIM files, including pulse
  numbers, fitted/fixed parameter flags and any instrument phase offsets.
- The final accepted observation list or manual RFI exclusions, so we can
  reproduce the cleaned sample rather than apply the S/N cut alone.
- Per-instrument EFAC/EQUAD and red-noise parameters, including their
  normalization and Fourier-frequency policy, plus any glitch conventions.
- Confirmation of the observatory/clock bindings and which EW release/version
  is included, so shared observations can be reconciled correctly.

We would cite the data release and paper and follow any applicable data-use
conditions. Please let me know if these products are already available in a
portal location that needs a different access level.

Thank you,
John Fadool
