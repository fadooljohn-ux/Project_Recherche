## Preparation and observed results

All 25 real-data preparations verified the published white covariance against
PINT, the repaired EQUAD convention and exactly one active epoch-DM window per
TOA. All timing designs retained full column rank. Calibration used 102,400
fixed-noise null simulations in total. Each target recovered one noiseless
100-day, 1,000-μs development injection with timing nuisance to numerical precision.
These checks are distinct from the observed searches. No engineering errors
required retries in this batch.

The 25 prepared calibrations were reused at launch. Thresholds span
26.120976–26.480293; median worst-phase amplitudes for conditional 95% on-grid
detection range from 45.842 to 241.145 μs between targets. These are fixed-model
sensitivity estimates, not posterior upper limits or planet masses. Masks,
thresholds, noise parameters and period scope were unchanged after freeze.

The strongest eligible statistic across the batch was J1326−6408 at 30.153556
days: **12.830413**, below its **26.204321** threshold. This is a sub-threshold
grid peak, not a detected orbital period. Every target returned NO_TRIGGER.
No reduced-χ² ≥ 2 noise flag was raised, which alone does not establish complete
noise-model adequacy or propagate uncertainty in the published noise parameters.
Planets below sensitivity, outside eligible periods or beyond the circular-orbit
model are not ruled out.

Per-target peaks, thresholds and noise diagnostics are retained in
[`observed-summary.csv`](../results/observed/tpa-batch03-20260908-observed-summary.csv).
Each observed run retained an independent full-design peak likelihood check.
Closeout verified the saved grids against frozen frequencies/masks, their
threshold decisions and likelihood agreement, without additional scans or fits.
