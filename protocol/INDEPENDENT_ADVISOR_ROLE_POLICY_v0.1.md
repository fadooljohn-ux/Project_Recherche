# Independent advisor role policy v0.1

## Authority

John Fadool is the sole project authority for scope changes, execution, data
access, milestone promotion, and publication decisions. Codex may implement and
enforce controls only within the authority John provides.

Fable and any comparable external model serve strictly as passive independent
observers. They may audit evidence, identify risks, test reasoning, and offer
recommendations. They do not authorize, unlock, approve, block, or veto any
project action.

An advisory `PASS`, `FAIL`, disposition, or sign-off is an audit opinion. It is
not delegated decision authority. Historical records that use words such as
`signoff`, `authorization`, or `verdict` must be interpreted according to this
policy: only the user's explicit direction supplied execution authority.

## Limited-use rule

Independent-advisor exposure is scarce and must be reserved for critical or
major milestone observations. Routine development, documentation, testing,
null-result closeout, and incremental releases use repository-native evidence,
frozen hashes, automated tests, scorecards, and explicit claim boundaries.

When external advice would materially improve a future milestone, Codex will:

1. consolidate all important questions into one bounded packet;
2. explain why the audit is worth the limited exposure;
3. obtain John's agreement before requesting it;
4. treat the returned findings as recommendations; and
5. present any proposed response or change to John under his sole authority.

Fable availability is never a standing prerequisite for continued project
work. A milestone may pause only because John requires an outside audit or
because the internal evidence itself does not support proceeding.

## Effective point

This policy applies prospectively after the completed Pilot 1 v0.1
post-execution audit. That audit is retained as passive independent evidence in
the v0.1 record.
