# NANOGrav binaries, batch 01: seven searched, one held

**20 November 2026. Owner instruction: "1. Search the eight fully cleared"**
— the eight unsearched NANOGrav 15-year binaries with measured Shapiro terms
that passed the [development check](NG15_BINARY_TOOL_2026-11-20.md). Tool:
`tools/recherche ng15-binary`, selected with `--shapiro-basis` (all eight)
and `--reexpress` (the five DD orbits). The 1% false-alarm allowance is
shared across the eight listed targets.

No crossing, so nothing went to the second stage; no candidate and no new

Things to know:

- **J1012−4235 was not searched.** It passed preparation, but calibration
  found no trial period that keeps enough signal power after the orbit and
  Shapiro columns are projected (65 TOAs, 3.4 years, range 380–614 days).
  The development check had listed it as cleared because that check stops
  at preparation.
- **J2302+4442 is a search in name only**: three trial periods between
  1,259 and 1,485 days, with twelve Shapiro columns (the maximum allowed).
  Its limit applies to that narrow band alone.
- **J1630+3734** has a null reduced chi-square of 1.44: the released noise
  model under-describes the data, so its limit is the least reliable of
  the seven. The others are 0.94–1.09.
- **First searches reaching 2,000 days in a binary** in this project
  (J1918−0642, J1600−3053), and to 1,500–1,650 days (J2017+0603,
  J1741+1351).
- The Shapiro basis leaves at most 0.088 of the TOA uncertainty unremoved
  (limit 0.1); the re-expressed models reproduce the released ones to
  0.0004–0.008 (limit 0.1). Column counts differ from the development
  check because the test points depend on a target's position in the
  batch; the bound is unchanged.
- Minimum masses are 95% detection sensitivities for circular orbits at
  the worst phase, a 1.6 M☉ inner system and fixed released noise.
- A first root, `ng15-binary01-20261120`, was prepared but its calibration
  stopped with an error at J1012−4235 before a hold could be recorded. The
  tool now records such a target as held and continues. That root was
  never frozen or run; the batch was prepared again in
  `ng15-binary01b-20261120` with the same selection, and no periodogram
  had been computed in between.
