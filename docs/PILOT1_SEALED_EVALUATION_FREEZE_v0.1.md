# Pilot 1 sealed null evaluation freeze v0.1

This gate authorizes exactly 500 disjoint evaluation nulls generated from the
frozen evaluation seed. Every case is scanned with the merged detector and the
locked threshold of 23.33426855482562 delta chi-squared. A trigger requires a
strictly greater value.

Threshold retuning, calibration-case reruns, observed-residual searches, and
candidate claims are prohibited. The outcome is the false-positive fraction
and its two-sided 95% Wilson interval. Progression requires a rate no greater
than 1% and an upper interval bound no greater than 2.5%, in addition to all
integrity and resource gates.
