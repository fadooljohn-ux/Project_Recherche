# Pilot 1 one-shot candidate disposition contract v0.1

1. A candidate exists only when a valid, integrity-passing run has a strongest
   unmasked grid statistic strictly greater than 23.33426855482562.
2. Integrity, resource-envelope, or ordinary/joint/full-covariance failures
   abort the run. They are not candidate evidence and do not authorize a retry.
3. Masked annual cells are sensitivity gaps only and never candidates.
4. Deletion diagnostics are mechanically labeled but wholly advisory.
   `DELETION_FRAGILE` and `DELETION_STABLE` have identical candidate authority.
5. Backend, frontend, observing-system concentration, residual kurtosis, and
   other predeclared advisory fields cannot create, rescue, demote, or veto.
6. No robust refit, alternate grid, alternate threshold, alternate mask, or
   second search branch is included.
7. The frozen report is published for candidate, no-candidate, or aborted
   outcomes with the mandatory stress-history context intact.
8. A threshold candidate is a calibrated follow-up candidate, not a discovery,
   companion confirmation, or mass measurement. It requires independent
   scientific review and external follow-up.
