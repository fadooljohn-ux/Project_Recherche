No crossing, so nothing went to the second stage; no candidate and no new
planet. Null reduced chi-square is 0.98–1.02; each peak passes the
independent full-design fit. The Shapiro basis leaves at most 0.076 of the
TOA uncertainty unremoved at its test points (limit 0.1), and the four
re-expressed models reproduce the released ones to better than 1e-4 of the
TOA uncertainty. The policy range ran to 400 days; grid cells above the
listed upper periods were not eligible. Minimum masses are 95% detection
sensitivities for circular orbits at the worst phase, a 1.6 M☉ inner system
and fixed published noise. Each `run01` destination is consumed.

Notes:

- Some targets carry more Shapiro columns than in the development check
  (for example J1514−4946: 4 against 2). The test points are drawn from a
  seed that depends on the target's position in the batch, so the smallest
  basis meeting the 0.1 bound can differ; the bound itself is unchanged.
- Calibration recovered 91–96 of 100 conditional injections at the stated
  95% amplitudes, consistent with 95%.
- J1614−2230 and J2145−0750 were each searched once before in NANOGrav
  data; this is their first search in MeerKAT data.

## The approved list as a whole

Thirty-two targets: **18 searched, all `NO_TRIGGER`; 14 held.** Three of the
holds were tested with the Shapiro treatment and stay held (J1909−3744,
J1327−0755, J1435−6100); the rest need a T2 translation, a nonlinear orbit
treatment, or have no period range. A hold is not a non-detection.
