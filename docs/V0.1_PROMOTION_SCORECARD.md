hard row below passes. No averaging or compensating score is permitted.

## Meaning of initial v0.1

Initial v0.1 is a calibrated, reproducible, single-target research pipeline for
circular timing signals between 30 and 2,000 days, subject to its empirical
annual-identifiability mask. It is eligible to prepare a separately frozen,
one-shot J1744-1134 analysis and to report a calibrated timing-amplitude
sensitivity surface.

Initial v0.1 is not a multi-pulsar survey, eccentric-orbit search, population
analysis, or discovery authorization. The Python package's existing semantic
version is a build identifier and does not satisfy this promotion gate.

## Promotion outcomes

- **PASS:** every row passes; create an initial-v0.1 release freeze and review
  record before any observed-residual search.
- **FAIL:** one or more completed hard rows fail; retain all results and open a
  corrective protocol amendment.
- **BLOCKED:** required metrics are absent or invalid; do not infer a pass.
