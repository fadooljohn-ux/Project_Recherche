# MeerKAT binaries, batch 03: two re-expressed targets searched

**20 November 2026. Owner instruction: "Yes search both"** — J1933−6211 and
J2322−2650, the two held targets that the
[orbit re-expression](ORBIT_REEXPRESSION_2026-11-20.md) clears. Same tool,
checks and rules as [batch 01](MPTA_BINARY01_2026-11-19.md) and
[batch 02](MPTA_BINARY02_2026-11-19.md), selected with `--reexpress`. The 1%
false-alarm allowance is shared across the two targets.

## Result

| Target | Orbit model (released → searched) | Outcome | Eligible periods (days) | Strongest peak | Statistic / threshold | 95% minimum mass |
|---|---|---|---|---|---:|---|
| J1933−6211 | DD → ELL1 | `NO_TRIGGER` | 128.2–296.2 | 159.9 d, 0.50 µs | 4.52 / 18.17 | 0.0016–0.0033 M⊕ |
| J2322−2650 | DD → ELL1 | `NO_TRIGGER` | 30.0–285.6 | 54.8 d, 0.99 µs | 7.42 / 21.36 | 0.0026–0.011 M⊕ |

No crossing, so nothing went to the second stage; no candidate and no new
planet. Null reduced chi-square is 1.01 for both; each peak passes the
independent full-design fit. The re-expressed model reproduces the released
one to about 1e-7 of the TOA uncertainty in both. Thresholds are lower than
in batch 02 because the allowance is shared across 2 targets, not 26.
The policy range ran to 400 days; grid cells above the listed upper periods
were not eligible. Minimum masses are 95% detection sensitivities for
circular orbits at the worst phase, a 1.6 M☉ inner system and fixed
published noise. J2322−2650's known planet-mass companion is its inner orbit
(7.75 hours) and is modelled, not searched for. Each `run01` destination is
consumed.
