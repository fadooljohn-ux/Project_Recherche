## Inventory and selection

Source: [Keith et al. 2024](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.02051v1),
[TPA timing release](https://zenodo.org/records/8430591), DOI
10.5281/zenodo.8430591. Publisher MD5s were verified for the three downloaded
release files; SHA-256 bindings are in `selection.json`. Inputs remain outside Git
under `../Project Recherche Data/tpa-batch01-20260908`.

All 597 targets are new to the master register. ATNF 2.8.1 resolves B/J aliases
and checks known binary status. The present scope requires at least 1,200 days,
40 observing epochs and frequency coverage, supplied pulse numbering and noise
parameters, and no published glitches or harmonic timing convention.
**191 qualify; five are prepared; 186 remain eligible for later batches.**
406 unique targets are excluded from this scope. Overlapping reason counts are
404 short-span/few-epoch, nine glitch and six harmonic-convention exclusions.
Excluded targets remain in the inventory, not counted as searched.

The cross-match finds 228 targets in the saved JBO planet-search inventory and
none in the saved NANOGrav 11 or EPTA DR2 inventories. Of the 191 eligible targets,
113 have no match in those three samples. Selection prioritizes that absence,
then a published-noise 100-day amplitude proxy. No observed periodogram or
residual table was used to rank targets. The proxy is not calibrated sensitivity.

This prior-coverage comparison is incomplete: [Kerr et al. 2015](https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.06982)
searched 151 young Parkes pulsars, describing its sample as largely matching
Weltevrede et al. 2010. Exact membership has not been reconciled here. In
particular, J1709-4429 and J1057-5226 must not be promoted as never searched.
All five are absent from the three named inventories; this does not establish
absence from the literature. These are independent MeerKAT observations.

## Prepared scope and calibration

One circular 30–400-day grid per target, oversampling five, 4,096 fixed-noise
null simulations per target, and a conditional 1% allowance across these five
grids. Existing calibration applies its empirical and analytic threshold rules.
Masks require at least 20% retained power and condition number at most 1e10.
The allowance is specific to this batch, not cumulative across past/future work.

Total: 2,811 TOAs, 352 observing epochs. Amplitude values mean conditional 95%
detection probability for an on-grid circular signal, worst phase at each
eligible cell, summarized by the median. They are not posterior upper limits,
planet masses or sensitivity claims for masked cells or arbitrary eccentricity.
Published red-noise parameters remain fixed; uncertainty in them has not been
marginalized and observed noise adequacy is still to be assessed at execution.

The adapter uses the full timing model, published TIM pulse numbers, DE440 and
local BIPM2019/MeerKAT clocks. Free spin, position, epoch DMX and six channel
JUMPs enter the nuisance design. Published EFAC/EQUAD/ECORR and the 100-mode red
covariance are retained. No fitted red-noise waveform is subtracted.

Two actual import failures were diagnosed and repaired:

1. PINT scales EQUAD with EFAC. TPA equation 2 adds it afterward. The adapter
   translates PINT EQUAD to published EQUAD / EFAC and verifies the entire
   white covariance against PINT for each target.
2. PINT's generic TCB conversion shifts DMXR observing windows. These are site
   MJD selectors, so the adapter restores their published values. Every TOA
   must occupy exactly one active DMX window, and every DMX column must be used.
   The initial calibration with empty DMX columns was rejected at review.

The original failed preparation and invalid DM-window profiles/calibrations are
retained under explicit attempt names. `preparation-attempt01-dm-window-fail.json`
is superseded and invalid despite its original machine status. Only the final
`preparation.json` and its bound profiles/cache entries are current.

Validation comprised three focused parsing/unit checks, five real-data
preparations with white-covariance and DM-membership checks, and one noiseless
100-day, 1,000-μs injected signal plus timing nuisance per target. All five
recovered 1,000 μs to numerical precision. These are development checks, not
observed detections or a new formal qualification. Qualified `src/` and the
existing MPTA adapter remain unchanged.
